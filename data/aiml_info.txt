
CHARUSAT CSPIT — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML)
=====================================================================

Program Overview
----------------
- Program: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- Duration: 4 years (8 semesters)
- Medium of Instruction: English
- Intake: 60 students per batch
- Started: Academic Year 2022-23
- Institute: Chandubhai S. Patel Institute of Technology (CSPIT), CHARUSAT, Changa, Anand, Gujarat

Admission & Eligibility
-----------------------
- Eligibility: Completion of 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Biology / Computer Science as one additional subject.
- Entrance Exams: GUJCET and JEE Main are accepted.
- Admission Process: Centralized counseling and seat allotment through ACPC, followed by admission procedures at CHARUSAT.
- Required Documents: 10th and 12th mark sheets, entrance exam scorecards, government-issued ID, passport-size photos, domicile certificate (if applicable), caste certificate (if applicable).

Fees & Scholarships
-------------------
- Approximate Total Tuition Fee (4 years): INR 5,92,000
- Approximate First-Year Fee: INR 1,48,000
- Scholarships: Merit-based scholarships are available based on entrance ranks and academic performance.

Academic Structure
------------------
- Academic System: Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)
- Components: Core subjects, elective subjects, labs, non-credit courses, and projects.
- Attendance: Minimum attendance required as per academic regulations.
- Evaluation: Continuous internal assessment and external semester-end examinations.
- Project & Internship: Final year project mandatory; internships are encouraged.

Curriculum Highlights
---------------------
- Core Areas: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Data Science, Programming, and Applied Mathematics.
- Practical: Lab courses linked with AI & ML subjects, semester projects.
- Electives: Offered in higher semesters with specialization options.

Facilities & Resources
----------------------
- AI & ML Lab equipped with NVIDIA Jetson Nano kits and Xavier developer kits.
- High-performance GPU servers and computing clusters available.
- High Computing Lab in CSPIT A7 building, Lab No. 638 (2nd Floor).
- Access to central library and digital learning resources.

Placement & Career
------------------
- Placement Cell: Provides campus recruitment drives, internships, workshops, and career development sessions.
- Highest Package: Around INR 24 LPA in recent years.
- Average Package: Around INR 8.5 LPA.
- Placement Percentage: Around 75% of students placed in campus drives (varies by batch).
- Recruiters: Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Capgemini, IBM, Tech Mahindra, and other leading companies.
- Career Roles: AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, Software Developer, Analyst, and Research Associate.
